Pre-IVF Nutrition Focus
Priority 1
Folate & B vitamins
Dark leafy greens, lentils, eggs. Critical for egg quality.
Priority 2
Antioxidants
Berries, walnuts, colorful veg. Reduces oxidative stress.
Priority 3
Omega-3 fats
Salmon, sardines, flaxseed. Anti-inflammatory support.
Priority 4
Blood sugar balance
Protein with every meal. Avoid sugar spikes. Hormone stability.
Avoid: processed sugar, alcohol, trans fats, excessive caffeine (1 coffee/day max). These directly affect follicle quality and hormonal balance. You're building the environment for something extraordinary.

What to Expect
Week 1 — The Installation
High energy, new feeling
▾
Everything feels doable. You may over-perform. Enjoy it, but don't over-invest. This feeling is not the baseline. Focus on completing the anchors, not doing them perfectly.
Week 2 — The Dip
This is normal. Don't quit.
▾
The novelty wears off. The routine feels mechanical. Nothing has changed externally — the system is being installed. Week 2 is the most important week not to quit.
Week 3 — The First Real Test
Disruption will come
▾
Something will disrupt the routine. Use the minimum viable routine: water, one journal sentence, 5-min meditation. Don't abandon — reduce. If you survive week 3, you're building something real.
Week 4 — Quiet Momentum
You'll feel it now
▾
The anchors start to feel automatic. The morning feels slightly off when you don't do it. That's the signal. You'll trust yourself slightly more — and that trust is the foundation everything builds on.
✦
You're building two things simultaneously: a business and a body ready for IVF. This plan runs on one truth — you don't need more discipline, you need better structure. Discipline follows systems, not willpower.
This is your pre-IVF window. Every healthy choice you make in the next 8 weeks is a direct investment in what comes next. You are not waiting for IVF to begin. It already has.
Your Core Principles
  • 01Never miss twice. One missed day is human. Two is a new habit forming — the habit of not doing it.
  • 02Minimum viable routine on hard days: water + one journal sentence + 5-min meditation. That's it.
  • 03Named days, not numbers. You exercise Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday. Not "try to do 5 workouts."
  • 04One task per work block. Not a list. If you do that one thing, the day succeeded.
  • 05Shukily gets protected time, not leftover time. Tue & Thu, 3–4:30pm.
  • 06You're not building a routine. You're building evidence that you keep promises to yourself.
  • 07Saturday is flex. On any IVF-adjacent hard week, Saturday is automatically rest. 4 workouts is a full win.
Hidden Friction Points
  • !Decision fatigue in the morning. Eliminate all unmade choices: same journal format, same meditation app, workout already scheduled.
  • !"I'll do it later" doesn't exist with a 10am–2:30pm work block. Protect mornings fiercely.
  • !Pseudo-productivity: reorganizing lists, low-priority replies. Feels like work, moves nothing.
  • !Comparison spirals on low days. A 3-day break is not starting over. The system waits for you.
IVF Preparation Mindset
Remember this
The habits you build now are not separate from your IVF preparation — they are your IVF preparation. Sleep, stress reduction, nutrition, movement, and mental clarity are all inputs into your body's readiness.
